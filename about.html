THE KALDI STORY

Coffee Has Always Been Part of Our Story.

KALDI Coffee House was created from a simple belief: great coffee should bring people together.

Inspired by Ethiopia's extraordinary coffee heritage, we created a place where traditional coffee culture meets contemporary café life.

Whether you're meeting a friend, starting your morning, working on your next idea, or simply enjoying a quiet cup, KALDI is designed to feel like a place you belong.

Crafted with passion, The KALDI Team
ETHIOPIAN HERITAGE

The Birthplace of Coffee.

Ethiopia is widely celebrated as the birthplace of Arabica coffee. Coffee is more than a beverage here — it is part of hospitality, community and everyday life.

At KALDI, we honor that heritage by creating an experience that respects the traditions behind Ethiopian coffee while embracing modern coffee culture.
